A Night on the Dance Floor at Sunrise of Issaquah

While volunteering with the Universal Health Access Foundation (UHAF) often involves organizing health initiatives or community outreach, our team recently had the opportunity to support our local community in a different way: helping host a summer dance for the residents at Sunrise of Issaquah on Friday, July 24th.

The event was a collaborative effort divided into two shifts. In the morning, our first crew volunteered from 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM, working closely with the Activities Coordinator. They spent the morning organizing the space and hanging decorations to ensure the room was festive and welcoming for the residents.

At 4:00 PM, our evening group arrived just as the residents were preparing for the event. Before the music started, we had the chance to socialize with them, and we were even able to assist a few of the gentlemen with getting dressed and selecting a cologne for the evening.

Once the dance began, our primary focus was on ensuring all the residents felt included and engaged. We managed refreshments and spent time conversing with those who preferred to sit and watch. For the remainder of the evening, we joined the residents on the dance floor—holding their hands, offering physical support, and encouraging them to enjoy the music.

This event served as a strong reminder of our core mission. At UHAF, we recognize that while physical health is critical, mental and social well-being are equally important. For older adults, social isolation can be a significant challenge. By simply being present, sharing conversations, and participating in the dance, we hoped to foster the kind of genuine connection that combats isolation and supports overall wellness.

We concluded our volunteer shift at 7:15 PM. It was a highly rewarding experience, and we are grateful to the staff at Sunrise of Issaquah for allowing us to contribute to such a meaningful evening.

🎉 A huge thank you to our incredible volunteers who made this event possible: Nicholas K, Mihir N, Rohan N, Arnav J, Vaidehi J, and Armaan M. Your dedication, hard work, and positive energy made a real difference in our community!
