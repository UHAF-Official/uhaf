Expanding Our Impact: Late Summer at Sunrise of Issaquah

Over the past two months, our team at the Universal Health Access Foundation (UHAF) has continued growing our relationship with the wonderful residents at Sunrise of Issaquah. Moving beyond our earlier social gatherings, we’ve started blending hands-on event support with interactive educational seminars designed to foster lively discussions and keep minds sharp.

August 15th: Comedy Night & Community BBQ

We kicked off late summer by helping the senior center host a fantastic community BBQ and comedy show. Harish S, Armaan M, Vivaan S, and I spent a couple of hours assisting the staff by moving chairs, arranging the event space, and setting up the dining area. During the meal, we had the pleasure of serving barbecue, helping the seniors select their desserts, and escorting them to their tables. The best part of the evening was simply sitting down to chat and ensuring everyone had great company.

August 22nd: World History & Cognitive Dexterity

A week later, Vivaan S and I returned for an hour focused entirely on interactive learning and mental exercise. To spark historical memory and group discussion, I gave a presentation on the history of Dunkirk and the Invasion of France. Meanwhile, Vivaan ran a fantastic hands-on Rubik’s Cube workshop with a group of 7–8 seniors. Teaching spatial patterns and cube mechanics served as a fun, practical exercise to support fine motor dexterity, memory retention, and cognitive functioning.

September 19th: D-Day & Bellevue's Boom

In mid-September, Armaan M and I hosted a 40-minute educational double-feature. I covered the complex logistics and lasting historical impact of D-Day, while Armaan shifted gears to local history, detailing the rapid economic boom of nearby Bellevue. Exploring local history alongside world events gave the residents a wonderful opportunity to connect our presentations to their own lived experiences here in the Pacific Northwest.

September 26th: Road to WWI & Modern Economics

We wrapped up the month with an hour-long deep dive into history and finance. I presented on the diplomatic shifts leading up to World War I, and Armaan provided a comprehensive overview of U.S. economics from the 1960s to today. Armaan's economic history presentation sparked an incredibly vibrant discussion, with many residents eagerly chiming in with their own memories and perspectives on these historical economic shifts.

🎉 A massive thank you to Mrs. Reid for her ongoing support, guidance, and coordination! And a huge shoutout to our dedicated volunteers—Harish S, Armaan M, and Vivaan S—for donating your time, energy, and empathy to make these events so impactful.

Looking Ahead: What's Next for UHAF?

As we move forward, we have some incredibly exciting goals on the horizon:

  • Elementary School Outreach: Expanding our domestic programs to host health awareness and preventative care assemblies for younger students.
  • Holiday Events at Sunrise: We are planning 3-4 major festive events for the seniors at Sunrise of Issaquah, including celebrations for Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and the New Year!
  • Global Impact: Continuing to organize and fund international medical camps for communities in need.
